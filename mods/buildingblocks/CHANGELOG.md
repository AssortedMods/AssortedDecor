# Changelog

## 2.0.0

Updated to Minecraft 26.3, for NeoForge and Fabric.

- Added Poplar framed planks, beams, panels and parquet, with their slabs and stairs
- Requires Assorted Lib 5.0.0

## 1.0.0

- First release as its own mod. It used to only be part of Assorted Decor
- Worlds from Assorted Decor 11.x keep all of these blocks and items
- Colored siding is made with a paint roller, so its recipes come with Assorted Paint
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.3.0
