# Assorted Hangeables

Things to put on your walls. Wallpaper and wood or iron frames that you can cycle through and dye, a calendar that counts the days, a wall clock that shows the time of day, and neon signs with glowing colored text and an editor for laying them out.

Also included in [Assorted Decor](../decor). Worlds made with Assorted Decor 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
