# Assorted Colorizer

Adds colorizer blocks that take on the look of any block you brush onto them with the colorizer brush. They come as slabs, stairs, walls, fences, doors, slopes and pyramids, panels, beams and columns, chairs, tables, stools and lamp posts, and fireplaces, stoves, firepits and chimneys.

Also included in [Assorted Decor](../decor). Worlds made with Assorted Decor 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
