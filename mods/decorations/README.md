# Assorted Decorations

Small touches for gardens and courtyards. Clay and bone decorations that you place and click through to pick a shape, a planter pot you fire in a furnace and fill with dirt, sand, farmland and more for whatever you want to grow, and a fountain that sprays water into the air while it has a redstone signal.

Also included in [Assorted Decor](../decor). Worlds made with Assorted Decor 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
