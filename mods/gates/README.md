# Assorted Gates

Adds the castle gate and the garage door. Hang one from the underside of a solid block and it drops down to the ground, and gates side by side open and close as one. Blow the gate trumpet or click the garage remote to open them from afar, or use redstone.

Also included in [Assorted Decor](../decor). Worlds made with Assorted Decor 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
