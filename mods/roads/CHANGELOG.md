# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release as its own mod. It used to only be part of Assorted Decor
- Worlds from Assorted Decor 11.x keep all of the roadways, sidewalks, stone paths, tar and asphalt
- Painting roadways needs Assorted Paint and the roadway light needs Assorted Lights
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.3.0
