# Assorted Roads

Adds roadways made from tar and asphalt, with road markings, manholes and roadway lights, plus sidewalks that are quicker to walk on and stone paths.

The colored roadways are painted with the rollers from Assorted Paint, and the roadway light is built with an illumination plate from Assorted Lights.

Also included in [Assorted Decor](../decor). Worlds made with Assorted Decor 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
