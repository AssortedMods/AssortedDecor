# Assorted Displays

Adds display cases for showing off your items. They come in wood, stone, copper, iron, gold and diamond, and the resizing tool lets a case show one, four or nine items. There is also a two block tall museum display case with a placard you can name, and a cage that shows a mob turning slowly inside it.

Also included in [Assorted Decor](../decor). Worlds made with Assorted Decor 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
